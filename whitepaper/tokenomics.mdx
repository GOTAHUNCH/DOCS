# Tokenomics

## Token Overview

\$HUNCH is the native token of the Hunch ecosystem. Trading on venues happens in their own collateral (pUSD on Polymarket); \$HUNCH is the rewards, rebate and governance layer on top, and the collateral for Hunch-native markets.

| Property | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **Token Name** | Hunch Token |
| **Symbol** | \$HUNCH |
| **Total Supply** | 1,000,000,000 (1 billion) |
| **Blockchain** | Robinhood Chain (Arbitrum-stack Ethereum L2) |
| **Standard** | ERC-20 |
| **Launch** | After public launch; preceded by the points programme below |

## Points Programme

Before TGE, users earn **Hunch Points**. Points are the basis of the Community & Airdrop allocation and convert to \$HUNCH at launch.

Every point traces to a real, verifiable action. Trading points are computed from fills attributed to Hunch on-chain — not from anything a client can report.

| Action | Points |
| --- | --- |
| Trade volume | Points per dollar of filled volume |
| Winning positions | Bonus on redeemed wins, scaled by how long the odds were |
| Daily activity | Streak bonus on days with at least one trade, capped |
| Referrals | Share of a referred user's volume points once they start trading |
| Profile | One-off bonuses for linking X and Farcaster |

Tiers are based on cumulative volume and days active — not trade count — so they cannot be farmed with micro-trades. Points are an append-only ledger, snapshotted with a Merkle root for the airdrop.

<Note>
  Points weights are tuned during beta and published before the airdrop snapshot. Points carry no monetary value until TGE.
</Note>
