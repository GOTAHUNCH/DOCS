# Venues & Settlement

Hunch connects to prediction-market venues through a common adapter: catalogue, prices, account, quote, order, positions, resolution and redemption. Each venue below plugs into that adapter. Users see one feed; the routing layer decides where each card trades.

| Venue | Status | Markets | Collateral | Resolution |
|-------|--------|---------|------------|------------|
| **Polymarket** | Live at launch | Polymarket's full catalogue | pUSD on Polygon | Polymarket / UMA |
| **Kalshi** | Planned | CFTC-regulated event contracts | USD, or USDC on Solana | Kalshi |
| **Hunch Native** | Planned | User, creator and community markets | $HUNCH / stablecoin | Hunch resolution framework |

## Polymarket

Hunch is a participant in the **Polymarket Builder Program**. A Hunch user's swipe becomes a real order on Polymarket's central limit order book, carrying Hunch's builder code.

### How a trade works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Account">
    On sign-up, Hunch deploys a Polymarket Deposit Wallet for the user through Polymarket's relayer. The user's Privy embedded wallet is its owner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Session key">
    The user authorises a trade-only session key once. It can place orders for 180 days and can never move funds out.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Order">
    A swipe sends one request to Hunch. Hunch quotes the order book, signs a marketable order with the session key, attaches its builder code and submits it to Polymarket.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill">
    Polymarket matches the order on-chain on Polygon and pays the gas. Volume and fees are attributed to Hunch.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Resolution">
    Polymarket resolves the market (via UMA's optimistic oracle). Hunch redeems winning positions for the user, gaslessly.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Economics

| Item | Value |
|------|-------|
| Hunch builder fee | **1% of taker volume** (Polymarket's maximum) |
| Maker fee | 0% — swipe trades always take liquidity |
| Polymarket platform fee | Paid by the user to Polymarket, separate from Hunch's fee |
| Fee settlement | Accrues on-chain per fill, paid to Hunch's builder wallet |

## Kalshi

<Note>
**Status: Planned.** Kalshi is a CFTC-regulated exchange, which makes it the route to US users that Polymarket cannot serve.
</Note>

Kalshi runs its own builder programme — **Kalshi Builder Codes** — under which third-party apps route orders and earn up to 30% of the fees their users generate, plus any fee the app charges on top. Two integration routes exist:

| Route | How it works | Trade-offs |
|-------|--------------|------------|
| **Kalshi API** | Users hold a Kalshi account; Hunch routes orders directly | Per-user KYC, USD rails, full regulatory cover |
| **DFlow (Solana)** | Kalshi markets tokenised on Solana; positions held as SPL tokens | Crypto-native funding, no Kalshi account, settles in stablecoins |

The route will be chosen when the integration is scheduled. Either way it becomes one more adapter: Kalshi markets appear in the same feed, with jurisdiction routing deciding which users see them.

## Hunch Native Markets

<Note>
**Status: Planned.** Hunch's own contracts return here — for markets no venue lists — on **Robinhood Chain**, alongside the $HUNCH token.
</Note>

Venues cover the big questions. Hunch-native markets cover everything else: a creator's call on their own niche, a community's question, a friend group's wager on the weekend.

| Market type | Created by | Resolution | Stake |
|-------------|-----------|------------|-------|
| Personal | Any user | Creator resolves; counterparty confirms | Small, friends only |
| Community | Communities and groups | Community vote with dispute window | $HUNCH bond from creator |
| Creator | Verified creators and KOLs | Creator resolves; Hunch adjudicates disputes | $HUNCH bond, fee share to creator |
