# Executive Summary

Hunch is a mobile-first prediction market app that turns forecasting into a TikTok-style swipe. Users swipe YES or NO on prediction cards. Behind each card, Hunch routes a real order onto an established prediction-market venue — **Polymarket at launch** — and earns a fee on every trade it brings.

Hunch does not run the markets its users trade at launch and never takes the other side of a prediction. It is the distribution, social and rewards layer on top of venues that already have the liquidity, resolution and regulatory posture — with Hunch-native markets to follow.

## Market Opportunity

The global prediction market opportunity exceeds **$50 billion annually**, yet existing platforms remain inaccessible to mainstream users. Hunch addresses three critical barriers:

1. **Complexity** of traditional trading interfaces
2. **Friction** in wallet management and funding
3. **Fragmentation** across venues and jurisdictions — one app, the right venue for each user
