# User Experience Innovation

The core technical innovation is making blockchain invisible to users. A mainstream user experiences Hunch as a simple mobile app, unaware that each swipe is a real order on a prediction-market venue.

## Onboarding Flow

| Step | User Action | What Happens Behind the Scenes |
|------|-------------|-------------------------------|
| 1 | Tap "Sign in with X" (or email, Apple, Google) | Privy authenticates via OAuth |
| 2 | (Automatic) | Privy creates an embedded wallet — the user's key |
| 3 | (Automatic) | Hunch deploys the user's Polymarket Deposit Wallet through the relayer, gaslessly |
| 4 | (Automatic) | Trading approvals and a trade-only session key are set up with silent signatures |
| 5 | Fund the account | User sends USDC from any major chain; it arrives as pUSD, ready to trade |
| 6 | Start swiping | Every trade executes via the session key — no popups, ever |

<Info>
**Total time from app open to first swipe: under 30 seconds, plus however long the deposit takes to arrive.**
</Info>

## Funding

Users never need to hold Polygon tokens or know what pUSD is.

| Method | Status | How it feels |
|--------|--------|--------------|
| Deposit from any chain | Live at launch | Send USDC from Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum, Solana and others to a personal deposit address; it arrives as trading balance |
| Card and local payments | Planned | Apple Pay / Google Pay, UPI, GCash, M-Pesa and similar, converting straight into trading balance |

<Warning>
There is no free starting balance. Users trade with real money at real market prices — and win real money.
</Warning>

## Gasless Transactions

Users never pay gas fees. Polymarket's relayer sponsors wallet deployment, approvals, trades and redemptions.

| Perspective | Reality |
|-------------|---------|
| **User Experience** | Swipe, instant confirmation |
| **Technical Reality** | Order matched and settled on Polygon; gas paid by the venue |
| **Economics** | Hunch's 1% builder fee carries no gas cost |

## Session Keys

The key innovation enabling frictionless trading. Polymarket Deposit Wallets support **session keys**: a second key the account owner authorises to trade on their behalf.

<Steps>
  <Step title="One-Time Authorisation">
    Signed once by the user's embedded wallet during onboarding — silently, no modal
  </Step>
  <Step title="Trade-Only">
    A session key can place and cancel orders. It can never withdraw or transfer funds
  </Step>
  <Step title="Time-Limited">
    Valid for 180 days, then re-authorised with another silent signature
  </Step>
  <Step title="Revocable">
    The user can revoke it at any time; open orders are cancelled automatically
  </Step>
  <Step title="Zero Interaction">
    Every subsequent swipe is a single request to Hunch. Nothing to sign on the phone
  </Step>
</Steps>

This achieves the "swipe to predict" experience that makes Hunch feel like a social app rather than a financial platform — with a hard guarantee underneath: the only key that can move money out is the user's own.

## Wallet Architecture

Users have two addresses (invisible to them):

| Wallet | Purpose | Created By |
|--------|---------|------------|
| **Embedded Wallet** | Owner key: authorises the account, signs withdrawals | Privy |
| **Deposit Wallet** | Holds trading balance and positions; executes trades | Polymarket (deployed by Hunch) |

<Warning>
Always send funds to the **deposit address** shown in the app, not the embedded wallet.
</Warning>

## Withdrawals

Withdrawals are the one action that always uses the user's own embedded wallet. The app signs them silently when the user asks; Hunch cannot initiate one. Funds can be sent to any address on Polygon or bridged out.

## Comparison

| Action | Traditional Crypto | Polymarket (web) | Hunch |
|--------|-------------------|------------------|-------|
| Create account | Install wallet extension, write seed phrase | Email or wallet login | Tap "Sign in with X" |
| Fund account | Buy crypto on exchange, transfer to wallet | Deposit USDC | Send USDC from any chain |
| Make a trade | Approve popup, pay gas, wait | Set price and size on an order book | Swipe and done |
| Session management | Connect wallet each time | Stay logged in | Automatic via session keys |
