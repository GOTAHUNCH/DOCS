# Go-to-Market Strategy

## Distribution Philosophy

Hunch employs a **crypto-global distribution strategy**, reaching users through crypto-native channels that transcend traditional geographic boundaries. This approach leverages the existing infrastructure of crypto communities to achieve rapid, cost-effective user acquisition.

## Geographic Strategy

### Target Markets

Crypto-friendly jurisdictions with high mobile penetration:

| Priority | Markets |
|----------|---------|
| **Primary** | India, Philippines, Indonesia, Vietnam, Nigeria, Kenya |
| **Secondary** | Brazil, Argentina, Thailand, South Africa |

### Jurisdiction Routing

Because Hunch sits on top of venues, the venue is chosen per user. One app, the compliant venue for each region.

| Region | Venue | Status |
|--------|-------|--------|
| Target and secondary markets above | Polymarket | Live at launch |
| United States | Kalshi (CFTC-regulated) | Planned |
| Other regulated markets | Case by case as venues and licences allow | Planned |

### Excluded Jurisdictions

<Warning>
Hard geo-blocking implemented for regulatory compliance. Each venue's own restrictions also apply to orders routed to it.
</Warning>

| Category | Jurisdictions |
|----------|---------------|
| **Regulated Markets (until a licensed venue is live)** | United States, United Kingdom, France, Netherlands, Australia, Canada, Singapore |
| **Sanctioned Countries** | North Korea, Iran, Syria, Cuba, Russia, Crimea |

## Legal Structure

Hunch operates through a **BVI Foundation** structure, providing the flexibility required for global crypto operations while maintaining appropriate legal protections.

| Entity | Purpose |
|--------|---------|
| BVI Foundation | Technology development and token issuance |
| Operating Subsidiary | Day-to-day operations |
| IP Holding | Intellectual property protection |
