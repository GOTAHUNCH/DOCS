# Business Model

## Revenue Streams

### Primary: Venue Fees

Hunch earns a fee on every trade it routes to a venue. The fee is set within each venue's builder programme and paid to Hunch automatically on fill — Hunch never holds user funds or takes trading risk.

| Venue | Status | Hunch revenue | Paid how |
|-------|--------|---------------|----------|
| **Polymarket** | Live at launch | **1% of taker volume** (the programme maximum); 0% on maker orders | Accrues on-chain per fill to Hunch's builder wallet |
| **Kalshi** | Planned | Up to 30% of fees generated by Hunch users, plus an optional Hunch fee | Kalshi Builder Codes revenue share |
| **Hunch Native** | Planned | Platform fee on native markets, shared with market creators | Hunch contracts on Robinhood Chain |

<Info>
This model aligns platform incentives with user activity rather than user losses. Hunch is paid the same whether a user wins or loses.
</Info>

## Unit Economics

Modelled on Polymarket volume only — the venue live at launch.

| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) | $1 - $3 |
| Average Trades per User (Monthly) | 15 |
| Average Trade Size | $15 |
| Monthly Volume per User | $225 |
| Hunch Fee (Polymarket) | 1% |
| **Monthly Revenue per User** | **$2.25** |
| **CAC Payback Period** | **< 45 days** |

<Note>
Low CAC is achieved through crypto-native distribution channels (KOLs, points programme, venue ecosystems) rather than expensive traditional advertising. Kalshi fee share, native-market fees and premium features are upside on top of these figures.
</Note>

## Path to Profitability

<Steps>
  <Step title="Year 1: Foundation">
    Launch on Polymarket, prove the swipe funnel, run the points programme. Negative unit economics acceptable.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Year 2: Growth">
    Scale users, add Kalshi for regulated markets, launch Hunch-native markets and $HUNCH. Approach break-even.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Year 3: Profitability">
    Premium features, creator economy and staking rebates mature. Positive unit economics.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Year 4+: Scale">
    Expand to new venues and regions, launch B2B products. Strong profitability.
  </Step>
</Steps>
